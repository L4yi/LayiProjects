How do I book an appointment with a doctor?

Yes, simply visit our website and log in or create an account. Search for a doctor based on specialization, location, or availability & confirm your booking.

Can I request a specific doctor when booking my appointment?

Yes, our system lists all available specialist doctors. You can filter by name or department to book the specific physician of your choice.

What should I do if I need to cancel or reschedule my appointment?

You can cancel or reschedule easily from your user profile dashboard up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled time slot.

What if I'm running late for my appointment?

Please contact our clinic support desk as soon as possible. Depending on availability, we will hold your slot or suggest the next nearest open slot.

Can I book appointments for family members or dependents?

Yes, you can register multiple family profiles under your single parent account to easily coordinate and book appointments for your dependents.
